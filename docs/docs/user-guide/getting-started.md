# Getting Started with Primer

Ready to experience Primer? This guide will walk you through your first interaction.

## Prerequisites

- A modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge)
- A microphone for voice interaction
- Speakers or headphones
- Access to a Primer instance

:::tip
Use headphones to prevent echo and ensure clear audio.
:::

## Step 1: Load an Experience

Choose one of three methods:

1. **From Storage**: Browse and select from available experiences
2. **From URL**: Paste a link to an `experience.json` file
3. **From ZIP**: Upload an experience package

## Step 2: Grant Microphone Permission

Your browser will ask for microphone permission. Click "Allow" to enable voice interaction.

## Step 3: Start Interacting

1. Click the microphone button to start
2. Wait for the AI agent to greet you
3. Speak naturally - the agent will respond
4. Watch for visual elements (scenes, notes, slides)

## Tips for Success

- **Speak clearly** at a normal volume
- **Be patient** - processing takes a moment
- **Ask questions** if you're unsure
- **Use headphones** for best audio quality

## Common Issues

**Can't hear the agent?** Check your volume and browser audio permissions.

**Agent can't hear you?** Verify microphone permissions and that you're not muted.

**Experience won't load?** Check your internet connection and try refreshing.
