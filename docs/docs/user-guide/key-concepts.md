# Key Concepts

Understanding a few key concepts will help you get the most out of Primer.

## Experiences

An **Experience** is like an app that you load and interact with in Primer. Each experience defines what AI agents you'll interact with, what they can do, and what visuals and sounds are available.

## Agents

**Agents** are the AI personalities you interact with. There are two types:

- **Frontend Agents**: Talk to you with voice in real-time
- **Backend Agents**: Work behind the scenes on complex tasks

## Tools

**Tools** are actions that agents can perform, like changing scenes, displaying notes, playing sounds, or creating documents.

## Sessions

A **Session** is a single interaction with an experience. Your conversation history, artifacts, and progress are saved per session.

## Artifacts

**Artifacts** are documents created during your session - summaries, reports, guides, or any other files the agent generates for you.
